High-Protein & High-Fibre Meal Planner

No Bread (Except with Soup) | 3 Adults & 1 Toddler (2yo)

Nutritional Priorities

This revised plan maximizes dietary fibre and protein while removing standard bread from everyday breakfasts and lunches. Standard grains are replaced with high-fibre alternatives like Simple Truth Rolled Oats, Spekko Brown Rice, sweet potatoes, and legumes. Lancewood High-Protein/Double Cream Plain Yogurt and fresh eggs boost protein, while the 2-year-old child benefits from softer prep options and Oh My Goodness! whole-food snacks.

Monday

Breakfast

Simple Truth Rolled Oats prepared with full cream milk, mixed with 1 tbsp of chia seeds and topped with banana slices.

Toddler Note: Mash the banana and stir well. Chia seeds soften perfectly in hot oats, making them easy to digest.

Lunch

Rotisserie Chicken Salad: Shredded Checkers Deli Chicken, canned Simple Truth Chickpeas (rinsed), baby spinach, and sliced cucumber tossed in a light olive oil dressing.

Toddler Note: Chop chicken finely, slightly mash the chickpeas, and serve with soft cucumber slices on the side.

Dinner

Roasted chicken pieces (breasts/thighs) served with roasted sweet potato wedges (high fibre) and steamed broccoli florets.

Toddler Note: Chop the chicken, remove the tough sweet potato skin, and mash the flesh with a fork alongside tender broccoli tops.

Tuesday

Breakfast

Scrambled Simple Truth Free-Range Eggs cooked in butter, served with a side of sliced avocado and sliced cherry tomatoes.

Toddler Note: Mash the avocado slightly. Cut cherry tomatoes into quarters to eliminate choking hazards.

Lunch

Leftover chicken, roasted sweet potato wedges, and cold broccoli florets.

Toddler Note: A balanced, self-feeding finger-food meal for the midday routine.
30-MIN MEAL

Dinner (Supper)

Quick Mince & Veggie Skillet: Lean beef mince browned with onion, garlic, canned black beans (rinsed), and frozen mixed vegetables. Served over quick-cook Spekko Brown Rice.

Toddler Note: Ground beef is soft and easy to chew. Combine the mince, soft rice, and well-cooked vegetables into a moist bowl.

Wednesday

Breakfast

High-Protein breakfast bowl: Lancewood Double Cream Plain Yogurt topped with a handful of raw rolled oats, pumpkin seeds, and a drizzle of Simple Truth Raw Honey.

Toddler Note: Stir the oats and honey thoroughly into the yogurt to soften. Skip the whole pumpkin seeds for the toddler (choking hazard).

Lunch

Leftover Mince, Bean, & Veggie Skillet with Brown Rice.

Toddler Note: Reheats quickly and provides a robust source of protein and dietary fibre.

Dinner

Mild Chicken & Lentil Curry: Chicken breast diced and simmered with a mild curry paste, carrots, and canned brown lentils. Served with Brown Rice.

Toddler Note: Lentils are highly nutritious and naturally soft when cooked into the curry. Mash with rice.

Thursday

Breakfast

Warm Rolled Oats cooked with grated red apple, cinnamon, and a spoonful of Simple Truth Smooth Peanut Butter.

Toddler Note: Apple gratings melt into the oats when cooked. Peanut butter adds protein and essential healthy fats.

Lunch

Leftover Mild Chicken & Lentil Curry with Brown Rice.

Toddler Note: Ensure the curry is moist; add a splash of warm water or milk when reheating if too dry.

Dinner

Grilled bunless beef patties (made from Checkers Fresh Beef Patties) topped with melted Cheddar, served alongside a leafy green salad with sliced avocado.

Toddler Note: Serve small, bite-sized pieces of patty topped with cheese, alongside soft avocado cubes.

Friday

Breakfast

Baked Egg Muffins: Eggs whisked with chopped spinach, chopped ham, and grated Cheddar, baked in a muffin tin.

Toddler Note: Soft, bouncy, and highly portable. Perfect for toddler self-feeding.

Lunch

Loaded Sweet Potatoes: Halved sweet potatoes baked and stuffed with canned shredded tuna mixed with a little mayonnaise and sweetcorn.

Toddler Note: Mash the sweet potato flesh together with the tuna and sweetcorn for an easily swallowed meal.

Dinner

Baked Sea Harvest Hake Fillets, served with a side of warm buttered baby potatoes and steamed peas.

Toddler Note: Flake the hake carefully to ensure no bones are present. Mash baby potatoes and mix with peas.

Saturday

Breakfast

Banana & Oat Pancakes: Flapjacks made by blending 2 eggs, 1 ripe banana, and half a cup of rolled oats (no flour, no added sugar).

Toddler Note: Naturally sweet and high in fibre. Serve cut into small strips.

Lunch

Tuna, egg, and bean salad: Shredded tuna, hard-boiled eggs, canned chickpeas, baby spinach, and tomatoes tossed together.

Toddler Note: Serve chopped egg, tuna, and slightly crushed chickpeas on a plate separately.

Dinner

Traditional Braai: Grilled Checkers Championship Boerewors, served alongside a warm, high-protein Three-Bean Salad (mildly dressed with olive oil and herbs).

Toddler Note: Finely chop the boerewors (remove casing). Lightly mash the canned beans from the salad for easier chewing.

Sunday

Breakfast

Omelette cooked with sliced mushrooms, spinach, and grated Lancewood Cheddar.

Toddler Note: Chop mushrooms and spinach extremely fine before cooking so they blend smoothly into the egg.

Lunch

Spelt Macaroni Salad: Leftover boiled Simple Truth Spelt Macaroni tossed with diced chicken breasts, cherry tomatoes, and peas.

Toddler Note: Spelt is highly fibrous and has a nutty flavour. Ensure tomatoes are quartered.
BREAD ALLOWED

Dinner

Thick, warm Butternut & Red Lentil Soup (high protein and fibre), served with toasted Sasko Premium Bread for dipping.

Toddler Note: Offer toasted bread "soldiers" to dip in the soup. Lentils blended into the soup provide smooth texture and high fibre.
